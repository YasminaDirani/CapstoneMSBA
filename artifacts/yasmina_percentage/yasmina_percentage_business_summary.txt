Executive Decision Logic
========================

When we auto-decide:
- Auto-zero only when validated stage-1 P(any aid) <= 0.05, the upper prediction bound stays <= 10.0, confidence >= 0.50, and interval width <= 18.0.
- Auto-award only when validated stage-1 P(any aid) >= 0.75, predicted aid is positive, confidence >= 0.50, and interval width <= 18.0.

When we review:
- Any case in a fairness manual-review subgroup goes to committee review.
- Any low-confidence, high-uncertainty, or middle-probability case goes to review.
- Moderately risky subgroups keep automation only under tighter thresholds (Moderately risky subgroups keep automation available, but only under stricter probability, confidence, and uncertainty thresholds.).

Errors that matter most:
- Harmful auto-zero: the model says no aid but the student actually received nonzero aid.
- Harmful auto-award: an automated award misses by more than 10.0 percentage points.
- Severe auto-award: an automated award misses by more than 20.0 points.

Why these thresholds:
- Error limits are tied to the historical 5.0-point award grid.
- Fairness gaps trigger at MAE +2.50, bias 5.00, or rate-gap 0.14, using validation dispersion rather than arbitrary fixed cutoffs.
- Probability thresholds were chosen by validation search; selected policy rank: 1.
- Calibration check: A bin near 0.80 predicted probability averaged 0.75 predicted vs 0.76 observed nonzero aid.
- Temporal holdout trained on [2025], tuned on [2026], and tested on [2027] with MAE 5.54 and R2 0.568.

Current held-out result:
- Auto-zero 37, auto-award 8, review 425.
- Harmful auto-zero 0, harmful auto-award 1, severe auto-award 0.
